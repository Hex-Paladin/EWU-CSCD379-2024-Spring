## 🎯 How to Use These Documents

### Before the Interview
1. **Read PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md** thoroughly to understand everything you accomplished
2. **Review INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md** the day before your interview
3. **Practice your elevator pitch** from the interview guide
4. **Prepare 3-4 technical stories** about challenges you faced
5. **Have the live app ready** to demonstrate features

### During the Interview
1. **Keep INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md** nearby for quick reference (if virtual)
2. **Use specific examples** from the code samples provided
3. **Reference the live URLs** to show the working application
4. **Connect features to skills** employers are looking for

### Interview Day Checklist
- [ ] Review INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md (15 minutes)
- [ ] Test the live application URL works
- [ ] Have 3 technical challenge stories ready
- [ ] Prepare questions about their tech stack
- [ ] Be ready to discuss any technology on your resume

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Be Specific:** Don't just say "I used Vue" - say "I used Vue 3's Composition API with TypeScript to build reusable components with reactive state management"

2. **Show Problem-Solving:** Focus on challenges and how you solved them, not just features you built

3. **Demonstrate Learning:** Show that you understand *why* you made technical decisions, not just *what* you built

4. **Connect to Business Value:** Explain how features benefit users, not just how they work technically

5. **Be Honest:** If you learned something during the project, say so. Growth mindset is valuable!
